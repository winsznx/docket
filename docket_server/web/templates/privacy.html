Only what it takes to call you.

Docket needs to know your place in line and how to reach you. It doesn't ask for anything else.

What's stored and for how long

For each ticket, Docket keeps your initials or first name if you give them, your place in line and the times your ticket changed. If you choose text alerts, it also keeps your phone number. Names and phone numbers are deleted the day after your visit.

How phone numbers are protected

Your number is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it's stored. The desk only ever sees the last four digits, and only the part of the server that sends texts can decrypt it.

What the report leaves out

The clinic owner can download the day as a CSV file with ticket times and waits. It has no names and no phone numbers.

Who can see a ticket

Anyone with the link. The link is the key to your ticket, so anyone who has it can see your place and take you out of the line. Your ticket page says so too. If a link gets out, the desk can move your ticket to a new one.

How to leave

Tap Leave line on your ticket and confirm. Your place is released straight away. Your name and number are deleted the day after, the same as for every other ticket.

No medical fields, ever

Docket never asks why you're at the clinic. There's nowhere to type a diagnosis, and when the desk gives someone priority, the reason field asks for no medical details.
