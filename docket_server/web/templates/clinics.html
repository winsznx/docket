Runs on the desk you already have.

Docket runs in the browser on the computer or tablet at your front desk. The board runs on any TV with a browser. There's nothing to buy and nothing to install.

The desk

The receptionist sees who's been called, their countdown and whether the alert reached their phone. Call next is the biggest button on the screen, and when it can't call anyone it says why.

The waiting-room board

Open the board on the TV in the waiting room. It calls every number aloud, shows which room to go to and keeps a QR code on screen so people can join from their seat.

Setup for the owner

Set your hours, lines and rooms. Print a poster with a QR code for each line, invite staff with a link, and read the end-of-day report with waits and arrivals by the hour.

Walk-ins the desk adds

Add walk-in puts someone in line from the desk and shows a QR code they can scan to follow their place. Without a phone they still get a number, and the board calls it aloud.

Priority with a reason

Add priority puts someone ahead of the normal line, and the desk has to say why. The reason stays with the ticket in the queue and the audit log, and the form asks for no medical details.

Transfer to Lab

When a consultation ends with a test, Transfer moves the patient into the Lab line. Their phone follows the new ticket on its own.
